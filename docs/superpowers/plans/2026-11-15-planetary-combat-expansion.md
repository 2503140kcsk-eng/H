# Starlight Wing — Planetary Combat Expansion

Date: 2026-11-15  
Target branch: `feat/planetary-combat-expansion`  
Parent: `feature/starlight-wing-mvp`

## Goal

Extend the existing iPhone-first Three.js shooter with a meaningful second progression loop without adding a paid backend, remote account, or third-party asset dependency. Keep the existing save envelope at version 1 and preserve older local records and JSON backups.

## Delivered in this slice

### Combat systems
- Four weapon profiles: Photon Pulse, Prism Laser, Comet Homing, and Nova Piercer.
- Two persistent weapon slots with a touch-sized in-run switch control.
- Laser and missile projectiles orient to their velocity; homing rounds steer toward the nearest current target.
- Piercing ammunition can pass through up to two targets. Each projectile may hit a boss at most once.
- Weapon unlock cost and equipment state are validated at the save boundary.

### Pilots
- Lumi, Noa and Rin now expose named skills, personal story introductions, planet briefing lines and animation styles.
- Noa's special attack adds a short Aegis shield response; Rin's adds a short movement/fire overdrive. Lumi retains the baseline nova burst.
- Hangar avatars have pilot-specific idle animation; in-flight wings and cockpit lighting animate with the selected pilot style.
- Full-body VRM characters, voice acting and skill trees remain separate future work.

### Planet events and in-sortie radio
- Each planet now schedules a tactical event at 16–20 seconds, before the first boss entry.
- Aurora: temporary 1.28x weapon damage (Starlight Resonance).
- Pyra: enemy projectile travel accelerates by 1.28x (Gravity Shear).
- Noctis: hostile bullets are cleared and a duration-matched shield is activated (Archive Ward).
- Each event has a pilot-specific line, a queued comms panel, and a completion reward of 650 score + 12 shards.
- Added a Playwright run test that observes initial pilot radio, event activation and completion.

### Campaign and bosses
- Three sequentially unlocked planetary stages with unique palette, briefing, pilot dialogue, mission log and boss.
- Three distinct boss forms/patterns: fan barrage, seeking missiles and crossfire.
- Boss core weakpoint opens on a repeating telegraphed cycle. Hits during that window apply each boss's profile-specific multiplier.
- Boss clears grant profile-specific shards and score. Endless mode cycles the three bosses.
- Boss collection and planetary progression are local.

### Audio and presentation
- Original note sequences generated by Web Audio; normal and boss patterns switch with encounter state.
- Launch, warning, weakpoint, shield, special, victory and defeat cues.
- Existing particle explosions are supplemented by bounded camera shake.
- No copyrighted recording or third-party sound file is bundled.

### Completion and migration
- Six local achievements, a boss archive, and top-ten device-local scores.
- JSON backup persists equipment, completed planets, achievements, boss archive and scores.
- New fields have defaults; backup format remains version 1.
- Save validation filters unknown IDs and non-contiguous planet completion flags.
- Playwright covers a complete export/import between separate browser contexts. This simulates a new browser profile, not a physical iPhone-to-iPhone transfer.

## Architecture and cost guardrails

- Three.js remains the sole runtime 3D engine.
- Collision remains bounded custom distance checks; Rapier and three-mesh-bvh are not justified by the current arena.
- Postprocessing remains off by default until real-device frame-time measurements justify it.
- Dexie + Zod remain local persistence and input-validation boundaries.
- PWA/Workbox remain via vite-plugin-pwa; no duplicate service worker.
- Supabase, Vercel Functions and online leaderboards are excluded from this client-only milestone.
- No Canva/Adobe paid credits or external model/music packs are required.
- Context7 was used to check Three.js animation and resource disposal patterns. Figma is View-only; no Figma write is claimed. The Superpowers connector is not callable in this session; its workflow is represented in these docs and CI tasks.

## Preview caveat

The StackBlitz link is a source-backed test route, not a guaranteed iPhone deployment. The current WebContainers browser guide (updated October 2026) lists desktop Chromium as fully supported and Safari as beta; cross-origin-isolation limitations can affect projects in non-Chromium browsers. On iOS, memory limits add another practical risk. A stable static-host preview (for example, Pages) remains a separate deployment task; no Vercel project or paid hosting was created for this milestone.
